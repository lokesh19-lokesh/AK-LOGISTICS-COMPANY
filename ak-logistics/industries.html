Industries We Serve

Tailored 3PL warehousing, freight, and distribution strategies engineered around the specific compliance, handling, and speed requirements of key economic sectors in Saudi Arabia.

Industry Specialization

Logistics Engineered for Sector Realities

Different products require distinct supply chain workflows. AK Logistics Company designs handling protocols, transport routes, and inventory practices tailored to your commercial industry.

Retail & E-commerce

Rapid fulfillment, order batch picking, custom protective packaging, and reliable last-mile delivery to shopping centers, storefronts, and commercial doorsteps. We also manage organized customer returns and exchanges to preserve brand loyalty.

Store Restocking Last-Mile Parcels Returns Handling

Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)

High-turnover pallet storage, batch and lot tracking, fast cross-docking, and scheduled store replenishment runs. Our Riyadh distribution hub ensures supermarket and hypermarket shelves across Saudi Arabia remain steadily supplied.

Batch/Lot Control High Velocity Palletizing Daily Restock

Manufacturing

Inbound raw material logistics coordination, vendor staging, buffer warehouse storage in Riyadh, and scheduled delivery of production parts to industrial plants across Riyadh Second Industrial City and regional factories.

Buffer Stock Plant Inbound Finished Goods Freight

Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals

Clean, disciplined storage conditions, sensitive medical equipment handling, and prioritized transport to clinics, hospitals, and pharmacies with strict verification of cargo seals and intact shipping manifests.

Clean Custody Priority Hospital Runs Strict Verification

Electronics & Technology

Secure, monitored warehousing with serial number tracking, anti-static handling precautions, protective cushioning, and controlled distribution to electronics retailers, service centers, and commercial technology clients.

Serial Tracking High-Security Bays Warranty Returns

Food & Beverage (Dry & Packaged)

Palletized storage and distribution for packaged dry foodstuffs, beverages, and canned goods. We enforce FIFO (First-In, First-Out) rotation to maintain product freshness and ensure timely supply to regional wholesalers and grocery distributors.

FIFO Rotation Sanitized Racks Wholesale Delivery

Industrial Equipment

Heavy-duty pallet and bulk storage for factory machinery, spare parts, industrial pumps, and electrical assemblies, supported by certified forklift operators and specialized flatbed or enclosed road transport.

Heavy Cargo Bays Rigging & Lashing Site Delivery

Construction Materials

Bulk staging and scheduled job-site freight transportation for architectural fixtures, packaged building chemicals, electrical conduits, hardware, and safety gear supporting active construction projects across Riyadh and the Kingdom.

Job-Site Staging FTL Flatbeds Scheduled Delivery
