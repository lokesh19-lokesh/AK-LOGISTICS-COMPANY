Practical Operational Insight

Data-Backed Solutions to Enhance Supply Chain Performance

Operational bottlenecks, disordered warehouse floor plans, and unoptimized trucking routes silently erode commercial profit margins.

AK Logistics Company provides pragmatic logistics consulting built on real-world execution across the Saudi logistics landscape. Rather than delivering abstract theoretical reports, our advisory team evaluates your physical storage facilities, distribution costs, and material handling workflows to recommend actionable improvements.

Whether you are establishing a new distribution center in Riyadh, reconfiguring existing racking for higher storage density, or rationalizing transport routes across Saudi provinces, we guide your operational transformation step-by-step.

Advisory Pillars

Consulting Services & Capabilities

Targeted evaluation and implementation support across four core operational areas.

Supply Chain Analysis

Comprehensive audit of current freight spend, transit lead times, supplier reliability, and inventory turnover to uncover operational waste and cost drivers.

Warehouse Setup & Optimization

Facility layout engineering, selective vs. drive-in racking calculations, staging zone design, and material handling traffic patterns to maximize cubic space usage.

Process Improvement & Re-Engineering

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) development for receiving docks, pick-pack stations, quality control gates, and dispatch loading routines.

Logistics Performance Reporting

Design of practical KPI scorecards measuring on-time in-full (OTIF) delivery, inventory accuracy, order fill rates, and cost per delivered unit.

Strategic Value

Measurable Results from Operational Optimization

Align your physical logistics workflows with sound commercial metrics.
