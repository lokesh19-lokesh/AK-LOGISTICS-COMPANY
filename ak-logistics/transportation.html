Freight Solutions

Seamless Road Transport from Riyadh to Across the Kingdom

AK Logistics Company offers comprehensive road freight capabilities tailored for commercial shippers, wholesalers, and manufacturers.

Operating from our central hub in Riyadh, we coordinate Full Truck Load (FTL) and Less Than Truck Load (LTL) shipments that link industrial zones, commercial centers, and regional hubs. Our transportation coordinators utilize disciplined route planning to identify optimal highway transit lanes, reducing turnaround times and fuel inefficiencies.

Whether you require recurring intercity shuttles, dedicated project freight, or scheduled parcel consolidations, our fleet management ensures cargo safety, consistent communication, and verifiable delivery records.

Key Capabilities

What Our Transportation Service Includes

Every freight service is calibrated to meet commercial transit specifications and road safety guidelines.

Local & Regional Transportation

Comprehensive coverage within the Riyadh metropolitan area as well as inter-provincial routes connecting surrounding business districts.

Full Truck Load (FTL)

Dedicated vehicle capacity for high-volume or sensitive commercial shipments requiring point-to-point dispatch without intermediate handling.

Less Than Truck Load (LTL)

Economical consolidation for smaller cargo batches, allowing you to pay only for the vehicle volume and weight your freight occupies.

Express & Scheduled Deliveries

Fixed time-slot scheduling tailored to dock receiving hours, retail restocking calendars, and critical production cycles.

Route Planning & Optimization

Systematic transit routing that factors in traffic patterns, road conditions, and multi-drop delivery sequences to maximize transit efficiency.

Cross-City Distribution

Direct line-haul transport linking Riyadh with key industrial cities including Jeddah, Dammam, Jubail, Qassim, and Medina.

Frequently Asked Questions

Transportation & Freight FAQs

Answers to common operational inquiries regarding our road freight solutions.

Full Truck Load (FTL) dedicates an entire vehicle exclusively to your consignment, making it ideal for large shipments, pallets, or direct point-to-point delivery. Less Than Truck Load (LTL) combines smaller shipments from multiple shippers onto a single route, offering an economical pricing model where you only pay for the space your cargo utilizes.

From our headquarters and consolidation hub in Riyadh, we provide local distribution throughout the Riyadh region as well as regular intercity freight lanes connecting the Eastern Province (Dammam, Khobar, Jubail), Western Region (Jeddah, Makkah, Medina), and Northern and Southern commercial centers.

We provide digital and physical Proof of Delivery (POD) signed upon cargo handover at the receiving dock, recording receiver identification, timestamp, and piece count verification.
