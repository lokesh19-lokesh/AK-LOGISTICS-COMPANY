Connect your warehouse directly to retail shelves and commercial doorsteps with dependable multi-location distribution and last-mile delivery services in Riyadh.

Direct Commercial Reach

Bridging Warehouses and Final End-Users with Precision

The final mile is frequently the most critical touchpoint in customer satisfaction. AK Logistics Company delivers organized distribution programs that ensure your shipments reach retail stores, wholesale buyers, or business doorsteps intact and on time.

Our distribution network connects our central Riyadh 3PL facility with commercial districts across Riyadh and surrounding urban regions. With vehicle types ranging from enclosed cargo vans to medium-duty distribution trucks, we handle daily delivery runs, store replenishments, and timed B2B drops.

Every drop is validated through transparent proof of delivery, providing your logistics and finance teams with verified delivery records for rapid billing and inventory reconciliation.

Distribution Capabilities

Retail & Wholesale Distribution

Scheduled replenishment deliveries to shopping malls, supermarkets, dealer networks, and wholesale commercial markets with dock-level offloading coordination.

Last-Mile Delivery Solutions

Final-mile parcel delivery to commercial addresses, offices, and regional customer locations managed with attentive handling and courteous delivery personnel.

Multi-Location Distribution

Consolidated multi-drop routing enabling single shipments from your central stock to be distributed across dozens of retail branches or branch warehouses efficiently.
