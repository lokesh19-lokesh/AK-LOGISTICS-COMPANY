Privacy Policy

How AK Logistics Company collects, manages, and safeguards commercial and personal data in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Jurisdiction: Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

1. Introduction

AK Logistics Company (“we,” “our,” or “the Company”), managed by proprietor Mohammed Ahsan Khan, is committed to safeguarding the confidentiality and integrity of personal and commercial data entrusted to us by our clients, consignees, vendors, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines our data governance standards in compliance with the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) issued under Royal Decree No. M/19 and relevant national regulations.

2. Information We Collect

In the course of providing third-party logistics (3PL), freight carriage, warehousing, and distribution services, we collect information necessary to fulfill contractual operations, including:

  • Contact Identification: Full name, company affiliation, job title, email address, and telephone numbers.
  • Shipment Information: Pickup addresses, delivery destinations, consignee contact names, phone numbers, and cargo descriptions.
  • Billing & Commercial Records: Corporate registration numbers, VAT identifiers, invoicing addresses, and transaction payment details.
  • Delivery Verification Data: Recipient signatures, photographic proof of delivery (POD), and timestamped receipt records.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, and usage analytics when accessing our digital website and quote forms.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process data strictly for lawful, defined commercial purposes, specifically:

  • Preparing freight, warehousing, and 3PL service quotations requested by users.
  • Executing domestic freight transit, cross-city dispatch, and warehouse custody.
  • Communicating delivery milestones, tracking updates, and exceptions to shippers.
  • Complying with statutory reporting requirements established by Saudi transport authorities and commercial regulators.
  • Maintaining operational records, billing audits, and customer support history.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosure

AK Logistics Company does not sell, lease, or monetize customer information. Information is disclosed solely on a need-to-know operational basis to:

  • Assigned transport drivers and warehouse personnel executing cargo handling and delivery.
  • Authorized regulatory and law enforcement bodies in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia where required by official statute.
  • Secure technology providers hosting our digital infrastructure, database backups, and telematics systems under contractual confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Security & Retention

We enforce appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect collected data against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Electronic records are stored in access-controlled environments, and physical delivery documentation is archived securely in our Riyadh facility.

We retain commercial and consignment records for the statutory durations mandated by Saudi commercial and tax law, after which documents are securely destroyed or permanently anonymized.

6. Your Rights Under Saudi PDPL

Subject to applicable legal constraints, data owners within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia hold rights regarding their personal data, including the right to:

  • Be informed of how their data is collected and processed.
  • Request access to or copies of their personal data held by the Company.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Request destruction or erasure of data no longer required for statutory or contractual purposes.
