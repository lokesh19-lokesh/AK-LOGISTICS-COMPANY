Stock Visibility & Accuracy

Maintain Optimal Stock Accuracy Across Your Operations

In accurate supply chain management, knowing your exact stock balances and batch locations is critical to avoiding stockouts and excess holding costs.

AK Logistics Company provides end-to-end inventory management processes within our Riyadh facilities. Every inbound container or pallet is verified against shipment manifests, barcode-scanned, and designated to secure warehouse storage bins.

When sales orders or transfer requests arrive, our trained warehouse personnel execute organized picking and packaging routines, minimizing order discrepancies and enabling your sales teams to promise deliveries with certainty.

Process Breakdown

Core Inventory Capabilities

Structured inventory procedures built around verification, location discipline, and reporting.

Real-Time Inventory Monitoring

Continuous tracking of stock inflows, storage allocations, and outbound dispatches to ensure records accurately mirror warehouse physical balances.

Stock Receiving & Inspection

Systematic dockside verification of inbound cargo against purchase documentation, checking packaging condition, piece counts, and SKU compliance.

Order Picking & Packing

Disciplined pick-list execution utilizing verified bin locations, careful packing, and protective carton sealing tailored to the sensitivity of your goods.

Inventory Reporting & Analytics

Structured inventory ledgers, cycle count reconciliation logs, turnover reporting, and aging summaries provided on scheduled frequencies.
