Beyond Standard Transit

Specialized Services That Enhance Product Readiness

Standard logistics simply moves a carton from point A to point B. AK Logistics Company provides specialized value-added services that ready your goods for retail sale, industrial assembly, or commercial distribution.

From meticulous cargo de-stuffing and palletizing to carton relabeling, promotional bundling, quality screening, and shipping document creation, our Riyadh operational hub acts as an extension of your own production facility.

By delegating secondary assembly, kitting, and compliance validation to AK Logistics, you eliminate the expense of extra warehouse machinery and reduce handling touches before products reach end buyers.

Service Scope

Core Value-Added Capabilities

Flexible auxiliary services designed around precision handling and regulatory compliance.

Cargo Handling & Palletizing

Container de-stuffing, shrink-wrapping, strapping, and re-palletizing according to standard Euro and industrial pallet specifications for safe transit.

Loading & Unloading

Supervised mechanical and manual loading utilizing certified forklift equipment, dock levelers, and securing equipment preventing cargo shifts.

Quality Inspection

Physical condition screening, packaging seal verification, batch code recording, and defect segregation conducted prior to inventory intake or dispatch.

Documentation & Compliance Support

Generation of delivery waybills, packing manifests, commercial transfer documents, and compliance paperwork required for Saudi trade checkpoints.
