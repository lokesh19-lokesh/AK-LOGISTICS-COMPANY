Terms & Conditions of Service

Standard terms governing transportation, warehousing, inventory management, and commercial third-party logistics agreements with AK Logistics Company in Saudi Arabia.

Last Revised: January 1, 2026 | Operating Entity: AK Logistics Company (Riyadh, KSA)

1. Scope of Agreement

These Terms & Conditions govern all quotation requests, carriage contracts, warehouse storage agreements, and 3PL supply chain services provided by AK Logistics Company (“the Carrier / Company”) to commercial customers, shippers, and consignees (“the Client”). By requesting a quotation or dispatching cargo with AK Logistics Company, the Client agrees to be bound by these provisions.

2. Quotations and Service Rates

  • All written quotations issued by AK Logistics Company are valid for thirty (30) calendar days from issuance unless expressly specified otherwise in writing.
  • Quotations are based on information supplied by the Client, including cargo dimensions, pallet weights, declared classifications, and stated delivery access conditions.
  • Rates are subject to adjustment if actual physical cargo dimensions, piece counts, or transit access constraints differ from initial quotation disclosures.
  • Value-Added Tax (VAT) is applied in accordance with the prevailing tax regulations established by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

3. Shipper Responsibilities & Cargo Declarations

The Client warrants and agrees that:

  • All consigned goods are properly marked, packaged, and secured to withstand the standard physical stresses of road freight transportation and warehouse mechanical handling.
  • Cargo descriptions, quantities, and addresses supplied on delivery notes and bills of lading are truthful, accurate, and complete.
  • No hazardous, prohibited, flammable, explosive, or contraband goods will be tendered for carriage or storage without prior formal written disclosure and statutory regulatory permits.
  • Receiving destination personnel are authorized to sign delivery waybills and provide electronic Proof of Delivery upon cargo arrival.

4. Warehousing & Inventory Custody

Goods accepted for warehousing in our Riyadh facilities are held in clean, organized storage bays. The Company maintains systematic inventory ledgers and conducts periodic cycle counts. The Client retains ownership title to the stored goods at all times. Cargo removal or dispatch is executed strictly upon verified client release instructions.

5. Delivery Timeframes & Performance

AK Logistics Company exercises disciplined effort to adhere to scheduled transit timetables and agreed customer delivery windows. However, transit durations are scheduled operational estimates and do not constitute an absolute or unconditional delivery guarantee. The Company shall not be liable for incidental delays arising from circumstances beyond its reasonable control.

6. Claims and Inspection Procedure

  • The receiver must inspect cargo upon delivery and record any apparent external packaging damage or shortage directly on the Proof of Delivery (POD) document prior to signing.
  • Claims for non-apparent internal damage must be notified in writing to AK Logistics Company within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery completion, accompanied by photographic documentation and original manifests.
  • The Company’s liability for proven cargo loss or damage attributable directly to gross negligence is limited in accordance with standard Saudi commercial carriage regulations. Clients are advised to maintain primary cargo transit insurance for high-value merchandise.

7. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be held liable for failure or delay in fulfilling contractual obligations where such failure results from events of Force Majeure, including severe weather disruptions, highway closures, sandstorms, civil authority directives, national emergency declarations, or other uncontrollable acts of nature.

8. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These Terms & Conditions and all associated commercial agreements shall be governed by, interpreted, and enforced in accordance with the commercial laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with logistics services that cannot be resolved amicably shall be submitted to the competent judicial courts of Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
