About AK Logistics Company

A reliable, customer-focused Third-Party Logistics (3PL) and supply chain partner headquartered in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our Story & Purpose

Built on Trust, Efficiency & Operational Excellence

AK Logistics Company is a reliable and customer-focused logistics and supply chain solutions provider specializing in domestic parcel deliveries, warehousing support, distribution, and supply chain management.

Under the leadership of proprietor Mohammed Ahsan Khan, our company provides efficient, secure, and cost-effective logistics solutions that help businesses move their goods with confidence. Based strategically in Riyadh, the commercial hub of Saudi Arabia, we support organizations across retail, manufacturing, FMCG, healthcare, and industrial sectors.

Our approach is built around understanding the specific operational demands of each client. Rather than offering one-size-fits-all services, we configure tailored transportation routes, flexible warehousing allocations, and streamlined inventory workflows that reduce administrative overhead and accelerate delivery cycles.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

These ten fundamental values steer our daily operations, cargo handling standards, and customer relationships across all touchpoints.

VALUE 01
Integrity

Operating with complete honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct in every shipment, agreement, and client interaction.

VALUE 02
Customer First

Prioritizing client requirements with responsive communication, tailored services, and attentive operational support.

VALUE 03
Reliability

Maintaining dependable transportation schedules, consistent warehousing care, and disciplined execution you can rely on.

VALUE 04
Excellence

Upholding rigorous quality standards across handling, packing, documentation, and final delivery performance.

VALUE 05
Innovation

Leveraging modern logistics technology, digital reporting, and route optimization to simplify complex supply chains.

VALUE 06
Teamwork

Fostering close collaboration among drivers, warehouse personnel, managers, and client supply teams for unified outcomes.

VALUE 07
Safety

Adhering strictly to safe cargo handling, road transit security regulations, and warehouse workplace protection.

VALUE 08
Accountability

Taking complete ownership of shipments from origin to destination with transparent status reporting and proof of delivery.

VALUE 09
Sustainability

Optimizing routes to reduce unnecessary transit miles, minimizing packaging waste, and supporting reverse logistics recycling.

VALUE 10
Continuous Improvement

Constantly reviewing operational workflows, integrating feedback, and refining delivery systems for sustained performance.
